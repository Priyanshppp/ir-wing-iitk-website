Phase 1 · Preparation (12–18 months before)
~18 months before
Check Your Eligibility
  • Consult the UG Manual (Section 8.5) or PG Manual (Section 6) for exact requirements
  • UG: minimum 200 credits of coursework must be completed — this limit is strict and non-negotiable
  • PG: minimum 2 full semesters completed at IIT Kanpur before the exchange semester begins
  • Pay attention to the minimum CPI requirement, credit requirements, and any departmental or programme-specific conditions
  • ⚠ Caution: exercise care if planning exchange in your final semester — academic and administrative timelines may affect degree completion. Students must remain registered at IITK throughout the exchange.
~15 months before
Shortlist Partner Universities
  • Start by reviewing active partner universities and MoUs on the OIR website
  • Departmental compatibility: identify universities offering courses relevant to your field and academic interests
  • Academic calendar: check if the host university's semester dates align with IITK's — conflicts are handled case-by-case with DOAA and your DUGC
  • Course availability: review courses offered specifically during your intended exchange semester (not just general offerings)
  • Eligibility requirements: check the host university's own CPI or grade criteria in addition to IITK's minimums
  • Funding & expenses: explore available scholarships and estimate travel, accommodation, and living costs
  • Language requirements: verify whether courses are taught in English or require certified language proficiency
  • Consult OIR's archived factsheets from previous years and speak to seniors who've attended your target university
~12 months before
Course Mapping & Academic Planning
  • DE and OE courses can be mapped if there is sufficient content overlap — DUGC approval is required
  • DC (Departmental Core) courses can also be mapped if the content alignment is verified by your DUGC
  • Get course equivalency pre-approved by your department before departure — post-facto approvals are not guaranteed
  • Plan your IITK semester load around the exchange period to avoid scheduling conflicts
  • Prepare your academic CV and obtain an updated official transcript
Phase 2 · Application (6–8 months before)
Watch OIR announcements regularly
Respond to OIR's Call for Applications
  • Monitor the OIR Announcements page — calls are released semester-wise and are time-sensitive
  • Submit your CV and latest official academic transcript to OIR
  • Consult previous years' factsheets (archived on OIR website) for university-specific information
  • Speak to seniors who've previously attended your shortlisted universities for practical tips
Upon receiving nomination from OIR
DUGC / DPGC Approval & Course Finalisation
  • Prepare a detailed proposal: host university, specific courses, duration, and course mapping rationale
  • DUGC approval is required to finalise course mapping — this step is mandatory
  • Consult your advisor if there are any scheduling conflicts or calendar overlaps with the host
  • The host university's semester dates are fixed — any flexibility must be confirmed directly with them
  • Obtain written approval and a signed course plan from your DUGC/DPGC
After DUGC approval
SUGC / SPGC Senate Clearance
  • Forward the DUGC-approved application to the Senate Undergraduate Committee (SUGC)
  • SUGC reviews and grants a credit waiver of up to 100 credits maximum
  • Obtain DOAA NoC as applicable
  • Receive the formal SUGC clearance letter before submitting anything to the host university
Post clearance
Host University Submission & Pre-Departure
  • Complete all acceptance and nomination formalities as instructed by OIR and the host university
  • Follow the host university's application and registration portal instructions carefully
  • Confirm visa requirements and prepare necessary documents well in advance
  • Contact the host university directly to enquire about scholarships — some are subject- or department-specific
  • Tuition fees are paid to IITK only; you are responsible for accommodation, travel, food, and living expenses at the host location
  • Arrange accommodation (check if the host provides it), travel insurance, and health coverage
  • Keep copies of all important correspondence, approvals, and registration documents
Phase 3 · During Exchange
First week at host university
Orientation & Course Registration
  • Attend the host university's international/exchange student orientation
  • Confirm course registrations match your DUGC-approved plan exactly
  • Any changes must be communicated immediately to your IITK department and OIR
  • Keep copies of all registration confirmations and enrolment documents
Throughout the semester
Academic Performance & Communication
  • Grades earned abroad are not added to your IITK CGPA — you receive a course waiver for approved courses
  • You must pass all approved courses at the host institution
  • Maintain regular contact with your IITK department advisor and OIR
  • For academic or course-mapping difficulties, contact your DUGC, SUGC, DOAA, or OIR promptly — approach the responsible authority directly to avoid delays
Phase 4 · Post Return
Within 2–4 weeks of return
Credit Transfer & Documentation
  • Submit official transcript from the host university to OIR and your department
  • DUGC verifies courses and recommends credit transfer as per the pre-approved mapping
  • Credits applied under SUGC's pre-approved waiver — grades do not accumulate into your IITK CGPA
  • Submit an exchange experience report to OIR (mandatory)
  • If you decided not to proceed after selection: withdraw promptly through the prescribed process — timely withdrawal allows waitlisted students to participate. Communicate your decision as early as possible.

Phase 1 · Profile Building (year-round)
Build Research Profile & Identify Programs
  • Join a research group at IITK with a faculty member
  • Aim for publications, posters, or conference presentations
  • Maintain a strong CPI — most programs require 7.5–8.5+
  • Shortlist DAAD WISE, Charpak, SN Bose, and direct outreach targets
  • Read target professors' recent papers for research alignment
Phase 2 · Applications (Sep–Jan)
Sep–Oct
Prepare Application Materials
  • Write a strong, specific SoP / Research Proposal
  • Update research-focused CV — highlight projects, skills, GPA
  • Request LORs from 2–3 faculty (give 4–6 weeks notice)
  • Get official sealed transcript from academic section
Oct–Dec
Submit & Confirm
  • DAAD WISE: apply via DAAD portal (closes Nov–Dec)
  • Charpak: apply via French Embassy portal (closes Jan–Feb)
  • Direct Outreach: personalised emails + CV + proposal
  • After acceptance — inform OIR, get NOC, apply for visa
Phase 3 · Research Period (May–Jul)
During Your Research Internship
  • Set clear research goals and milestones with your host supervisor
  • Document progress regularly — builds your publication portfolio
  • Network with other researchers and attend lab seminars
Phase 4 · After Return
Leverage Your Experience
  • Request completion certificate and recommendation letter from host supervisor
  • Continue collaboration if possible — aim for a paper or technical report
  • Use the experience in your higher studies application SoP
  • Share learnings with juniors through the IR Wing

Deadline Alert: Research internship applications typically open in September for the following summer. Missing this window means missing the full cycle. Set reminders starting in August every year.

Phase 1 · Identify Opportunity
Find a Collaborative Project
  • Explore IITK's Collaborative Research Centres: Rice-IITK, La Trobe, UB, NYU Tandon
  • Ask your IITK supervisor about ongoing international co-supervised projects
  • Review active MoUs on OIR website for project-eligible partnerships

  • Discuss the project idea with your IITK faculty supervisor
  • Obtain written consent and define co-supervision arrangement

Execution & Reporting
  • Maintain bi-weekly progress updates to both supervisors
  • Document all research outputs meticulously
  • Follow both institutions' research ethics and IP policies

  • Submit project report to IITK supervisor and department

  • Credits registered under BTP/MTP in IITK grade sheet
  • Explore conference publication or patent if applicable

Phase 1 · Foundation (18–12 months before)
Start Early (2nd–3rd year)
Academic & Research Foundation
  • Maintain a strong CPI — aim for 8.0+ for top PhD programs
  • Join a research group at IITK for hands-on experience
  • Attempt a publication or conference paper
  • Identify your research area(s) of interest early
~15 months before
Standardised Tests & LOR Relationships
  • Start GRE preparation 3–4 months before test date
  • Book TOEFL/IELTS; complete language tests if needed
  • Identify 3 faculty who know your research work well
  • Engage through courses, projects, or TA roles
Phase 2 · Strategy (9–6 months before deadlines)
Research Universities & Draft Materials
  • Focus on specific research groups, not just rankings
  • Create a shortlist of 8–12 universities (reach, match, safe)
  • Begin drafting Statement of Purpose for each program
  • Highlight IITK research, coursework, and career goals
  • Update CV with publications, projects, awards
Professor Cold-Emailing (for PhD)
  • Write personalised, research-specific emails to target professors
  • Mention a specific paper of theirs and explain how you can contribute
  • Attach a brief research proposal and your CV
  • Follow up once after 2–3 weeks if no response
Phase 3 · Submitting Applications (Aug–Jan)
Aug–Sep
Finalise Materials & Request LORs
  • Request LORs from chosen faculty — give 6 weeks notice
  • Get official sealed transcript from IITK academic section (2–4 weeks)
  • Compile all standardised test score reports
Oct–Dec
Submit Applications & Interviews
  • Complete all university portals before their deadlines
  • Pay application fees (USD 50–100 per university typically)
  • Apply for scholarships: DAAD, Fulbright-Nehru, Erasmus Mundus
  • Prepare for interviews with admissions committees or supervisors
Phase 4 · Post Offer (March onwards)
Accept, Visa & Departure
  • Compare funding packages, research group quality, cost of living
  • Accept by standard deadline (typically April 15 for US)
  • Apply for student visa (F-1 for USA, Tier 4 for UK, etc.)
  • Obtain degree certificate and official transcripts from IITK
  • Arrange accommodation, insurance, and travel

Master Checklist for Higher Studies

  • GRE/GMAT score ready
  • TOEFL/IELTS score ready
  • Official transcript ordered
  • 3 LOR requests sent
  • Research CV updated
  • SoP drafted & reviewed
  • 10+ universities shortlisted
  • Professor emails sent (PhD)
  • Application fees budgeted
  • Scholarship apps submitted
  • Acceptance decision made
  • Visa applied

IITK Advantage: Admissions committees at top global universities are well aware of the rigour of IITK programs. Your IIT Kanpur degree carries significant weight — leverage your research experience, faculty connections, and the OIR's alumni network to maximise your application's impact.
